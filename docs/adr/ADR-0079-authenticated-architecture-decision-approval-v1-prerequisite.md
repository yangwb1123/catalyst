# ADR-0079: Authenticated Architecture Decision Approval v1 Prerequisite

## Context
ADR-0067 fixes every new ArchitectureDecisionRecord v2 at status proposed with null acceptance fields. Its owner and approver references are unauthenticated declarations. ADR-0059 fixes ApprovalRecord v1 but deliberately does not verify authority proof, separation-of-duty proof, revocation or effective approval. Neither contract can authorize or perform a proposed-to-accepted transition.

ADR-0057 and ADR-0058 demonstrate external root pins, disjoint key usages, domain-separated Ed25519 messages, signed-shaped policy, request and receipt artifacts, complete state and CAS relations. Their roots, keys, repo.read scope, CapabilityGrant and execution semantics are closed to their bootstrap purposes. This decision may reuse that pattern but cannot reuse or reinterpret those authority objects.

The first honest slice is an offline structural candidate. It can freeze prospective wires and check supplied canonical bytes, digests and relations. It cannot verify Ed25519, trust the supplied root, obtain current time or revocation state, sign or mint a receipt, make CAS durable or accept an ADR.

## Decision
Propose the independent Authenticated Architecture Decision Approval v1 prerequisite contract and only its dependency-free Python structural candidate core.

### Trust, proposal and policy relations
Use the existing generic forgeos.ed25519-domain-sha256/v1 SignatureProfile shape, but an entirely new unsigned root. The root has exactly one approval_policy_sign, approval_request_auth, approval_revocation_sign and approval_authorization_state_sign key and two through sixteen architecture_approval_sign keys. Key IDs, principals and 32-byte public keys are pairwise distinct. Service usages require services, approval usages require humans or operators and the request usage may be an agent, human, operator or service. A root self-digest proves only integrity of supplied bytes. Trust requires a future external exact root SHA-256 and epoch pin.

The proposal is embedded as exact raw strict ADR v2 bytes encoded with canonical unpadded base64url. Recompute its plain physical SHA-256 and its domain-separated body and self digests. All must equal ProposalBinding; status remains proposed and acceptance_id and accepted_at_unix_ms remain null.

Policy is exact-proposal only, valid no more than 24 hours, permits at most a five-minute request, requires threshold two through sixteen and always vetoes a reject. It maps every exact proposal approver_ref to one eligible approval key and every owner_ref to one principal. Eligible approvers must differ from each other, requester, service subject, every owner and every implementer. Required distinctions are approver_not_implementer, approver_not_owner, approver_not_requester, approver_not_subject and approvers_pairwise_distinct.

### Approval, revocation and ledger relations
Each embedded ADR-0059 ApprovalRecord uses L4 gate scope architecture-decision-acceptance-prerequisite-v1 with effect_id null, external_operator authority and an eligible architecture approval key. Conditions and risk_acceptance_refs are empty. Requester, implementers and all three ApprovalRecord distinctions equal signed-shaped policy roles. Embedded revoked_at_unix_ms is null. Bindings equal the policy profile and policy digest. Artifacts are exactly the proposal body, physical and self triples in canonical order. Approval IDs and approver principals are unique and records are ordered by approval_id. Both proof fields are prospective signatures by that same eligible approver key over the ApprovalRecord self digest under their separate domains.

The complete ledger contains the revocation sequence from one through high-water with exact prior links and monotonically accumulating revoked sets. Supplied request and receipt times must lie within supplied policy, request, approval and revocation windows. A supplied revoked approval or key fails. This is consistency against caller data, not currentness.

Idempotency keys are 16 through 128 closed visible ASCII bytes. Sequence one requires null expected_ledger_sha256; later sequences require a digest. The structural core checks request, entry and receipt sequence agreement but has no prior durable state against which to perform CAS. Receipt labels are acceptance_transition_authorized only when the supplied policy allows, no supplied record rejects and supplied approves reach threshold. Otherwise the label is acceptance_transition_not_authorized with exactly one reason using precedence policy_denied, then authenticated_reject, then insufficient_authenticated_approvals. qualifying_approval_ids is empty for policy_denied and otherwise contains every supplied approve approval_id in lexical order, including reject and insufficient outcomes. These are declared structural relations, not authorization. Receipt expiry is the minimum supplied validity bound.

The ledger requires contiguous entry and receipt chains, unique record-key digests, at most one authorized-shaped receipt per proposal binding, observed-time high-water, complete revocation state and exact top-level membership. Delivery is stored or exact_replay. Neither label attests that bytes were written, synchronized, atomically replaced, reopened, retained or protected from rollback.

All JSON uses forgeos.canonical-json/v1: exact compact bytes, duplicate and unknown rejection, raw UTF-8 key order, signed-int64 integers, no floats, depth 16, object width 64, array width 256, and no controls, DEL, bidi controls, surrogates, U+2028 or U+2029. The maximum bytes are 16 KiB profile, 256 KiB root, 64 KiB proposal binding, 1 MiB policy, 512 KiB revocation, 4 MiB request, 256 KiB receipt, 512 KiB result, 64 MiB ledger and golden, and 256 KiB proposal. The physical golden is canonical instance bytes plus exactly one LF; instance mode rejects that LF.

Independent NUL-terminated digest domains cover profile, root, proposal binding, policy, revocation, request, receipt, ledger and idempotency record key. Each self digest field is empty in its preimage. Request ID and digest and receipt ID and digest are jointly empty. Signed-shaped artifacts also empty signature_base64url. Future verification must use distinct policy, revocation, request, receipt, ledger, ApprovalRecord authority and ApprovalRecord SoD signature domains followed by the raw 32-byte artifact digest.

The public Python API exposes strict decode and validation, canonical JSON, self-digest helpers, proposal binding verification and deterministic golden loading. It exposes no signature verification, authorization, signing, issuance, storage, CAS, acceptance or transition operation. Success is the frozen STRUCTURALLY_VALID_AUTHENTICATED_ADR_APPROVAL_V1_CANDIDATE marker explicitly followed by no authentication, authorization, acceptance, persistence, effect, root-pin, time-currentness, revocation-currentness, CAS or durability attestation.

Fixture root, key IDs, public keys and signatures are test-only proof-shaped bytes. Any future non-test verifier or service must reject the exact fixture root and keys and consume its root pin, epoch, minimum revocation sequence and evaluation time outside the bundle. ADR-0079 does not provision its root or authorize itself. Future deployment must externally exclude this decision's own proposal identity from bootstrap authorization and prevent requester, owner, implementer, signer or approver self-bootstrap.

## Consequences
A later authenticated verifier and service gain one exact wire and adversarial corpus without the current checker claiming their semantics. Proposal identity, roles, ApprovalRecord profile, threshold, revocation and complete-ledger relations are deterministic and require no ambient repository, network, clock, credential or write.

Signed, authorization and stored are prospective wire-role names. In this slice signatures are canonical strings, time and state are caller supplied and the ledger is a caller-supplied snapshot. A structurally authorized-shaped receipt is neither authenticated nor authorized and cannot be an ADR acceptance ID.

No ADR-0057 or ADR-0058 root, Grant, repo.read scope, execution manifest, policy decision point, permission or effect is reused. ApprovalRecord retains its frozen effect vocabulary field only as ADR-0059 wire compatibility; gate scope has null effect_id.

## Validation
Run the checker with Python site packages disabled. Require deterministic golden reconstruction, one-LF framing, all physical pins, exact proposal physical, body and self binding, and the frozen negative marker. Validate the Schema offline with an explicitly registered ApprovalRecord v1 Schema; missing resolution fails closed.

Mutate every field, constant, domain and self preimage; duplicate and unknown keys; JSON form, UTF-8, controls, bidi, depth, width, integer and byte bounds; base64url tails; proposal bytes, filename, status and all three identities; root usages and distinctness; policy mappings, threshold and all SoD separations; ApprovalRecord gate, null effect, empty conditions and risks, proof profile, decisions and three artifacts; time and revocation windows; idempotency and CAS shape; receipt outcome, reasons, expiry and chains; result and ledger membership, high-water and uniqueness.

Replace a policy signature with different canonical 64-byte proof-shaped data, update only the enclosing ledger self digest and require structural success. This proves the Python core does not verify or claim Ed25519. Validate ADR-0079 itself as strict Proposed v2 with null acceptance fields. Require regular mode 0644, files at most 500 lines, functions below 50 lines and no bytecode cache.

## Limitations
This slice does not authenticate any root, key, principal, requester, approver, owner, implementer, subject, signature, proposal source, repository or publisher. It does not verify Ed25519 or SoD proof bytes, consume external pins, establish key custody, read trusted time, prove freshness or current revocation, resist administrator rollback or rotate roots.

It does not sign, mint or issue any artifact. Fixture construction is test reconstruction, not issuance. It provides no service, socket, HSM, remote signer, separate operating-system principal, durable state, fsync, atomic rename, reopen, CAS, replay or conflict endpoint.

It does not accept, reject, supersede, rewrite or persist an ADR; modify lifecycle fields; create an AcceptanceReceipt or acceptance ledger/view; register a route, capability, Grant, permission or effect; call forge accept; or close G0. ADR-0079 remains Proposed and cannot bootstrap, approve or accept itself. Production remains blocked on external trust provisioning, authenticated verification, trusted time and revocation, durable service state and a separate accepted-lifecycle transition contract.
