# ADR-0092: Decision Capsule Structural Replay Core v1

## Context
ADR-0090 closes a caller-supplied decision transaction, cognitive atoms and the embedded ADR-0088 operational attempt graph. It intentionally does not define a portable capsule for retaining that closure, a complete replay inventory, or a separately sealed comparison result. A later reflection report may need to cite such a capsule, but allowing the capsule to cite that report would create a digest cycle.

The missing layer is easy to overstate. Re-reading and resealing supplied bytes can prove exact structural equality; it cannot reconstruct external history, resolve world state, evaluate a model or rule, authenticate a result, establish evaluator independence, replay an effect, rewrite history, authorize an action, persist a decision, or act as a PDP or controller. A truthful contract must keep those claims visibly false while still preserving every failed, lost, retried and successful attempt already closed by ADR-0090.

The safe dependency is therefore a one-way four-object wire DAG. A complete ordered manifest is derived from one sealed ADR-0090 closure. A capsule embeds both without changing either. A separately sealed branch binds only their exact references and a fixed structural comparison result. An outer closure embeds the capsule and branch and may carry unresolved dedicated ReflectionReport ArtifactRefs. Reflection reports may point to a capsule, while only the outer closure owns the dedicated later-report attachment field. Upstream ADR-0088 ArtifactRefs remain opaque structural values; this core does not interpret their `artifact_kind` labels.

## Decision
Adopt four exact sealed objects: `StructuralReplayManifest`, `DecisionCapsule`, `EvaluationBranch` and `StructuralReplayClosure`. The digest DAG is `StructuralReplayManifest` to `DecisionCapsule` to `EvaluationBranch` to `StructuralReplayClosure`. The capsule embeds and fully validates the exact caller-supplied ADR-0090 `KernelDecisionReferenceClosure`. Validation blanks only that embedded closure's own `closure_id` and `closure_sha256`, recomputes its ADR-0090 seal and requires byte-for-byte object equality. It neither reconstructs nor substitutes any nested record.

`StructuralReplayManifest` has exactly nineteen fields: `api_version`, `artifact_receipt_refs`, `artifact_refs`, `attestations`, `canonicalization`, `capability_invocation_refs`, `decision_closure_ref`, `decision_transaction_ref`, `effect_replay_allowed`, `execution_receipt_refs`, `history_rewrite_allowed`, `interaction_event_refs`, `kind`, `manifest_id`, `manifest_sha256`, `operational_closure_ref`, `postdecision_atom_refs`, `predecision_atom_refs` and `replay_mode`. Its API and kind are `forgeos.aadm.structural-replay-manifest/v1` and `StructuralReplayManifest`; replay mode is `structural_validate_reseal_compare_only`; both replay controls are false.

The manifest is the unique complete projection of the embedded closure, not a caller-selected list. Decision, transaction and operational closure refs are exact. Predecision and postdecision atom refs retain ADR-0090 atom identity order after the exact source-phase partition, with at least one predecision ref and no more than 256 refs across both arrays. ArtifactRefs retain ADR-0088 canonical-byte order. ArtifactReceipt refs retain receipt-ID order. CapabilityInvocation and ExecutionReceipt refs retain attempt order. InteractionEvent refs retain attempt and logical-sequence order. Arrays are unique and their exact lengths equal the embedded arrays. Failed, lost, retry and succeeded attempts are all retained.

`DecisionCapsule` has exactly ten fields: `api_version`, `attestations`, `canonicalization`, `capsule_id`, `capsule_mode`, `capsule_sha256`, `decision_closure`, `kind`, `replay_manifest` and `result`. Its API and kind are `forgeos.aadm.decision-capsule/v1` and `DecisionCapsule`; mode is `structural_replay_manifest_only`. Its exact result is `STRUCTURALLY_VALID_DECISION_CAPSULE_V1 (exact caller-supplied ADR-0090 closure and complete projection of the embedded caller-supplied closure only; replay is validate/reseal/compare only; no effect replay or history rewrite; all thirty-two replay attestations are false)`.

`EvaluationBranch` has exactly thirteen fields: `api_version`, `attestations`, `branch_id`, `branch_mode`, `branch_sha256`, `canonicalization`, `capsule_ref`, `comparison_result`, `decision_closure_ref`, `effect_replay_allowed`, `history_rewrite_allowed`, `kind` and `manifest_ref`. Its API and kind are `forgeos.aadm.evaluation-branch/v1` and `EvaluationBranch`. Branch mode is `structural_validate_reseal_compare_only`, comparison result is `EXACT_STRUCTURAL_REFERENCE_MATCH_ONLY`, and both replay controls are false. The branch is uniquely derived from one validated capsule. It carries no candidate object and claims no evaluator independence.

`StructuralReplayClosure` has exactly ten fields: `api_version`, `attestations`, `canonicalization`, `closure_id`, `closure_sha256`, `decision_capsule`, `evaluation_branch`, `kind`, `reflection_report_artifact_refs` and `result`. Its API and kind are `forgeos.aadm.structural-replay-closure/v1` and `StructuralReplayClosure`. The zero through thirty-two dedicated reflection refs are exact ArtifactRefs, canonical-byte sorted and unique, and every `artifact_kind` is exactly `reflection_report`. This dedicated attachment array remains unresolved and appears only here; identically labelled upstream ArtifactRefs are carried through without interpretation. The exact outer result and checker success line is `STRUCTURALLY_VALID_DECISION_CAPSULE_REPLAY_CLOSURE_V1 (exact caller-supplied DecisionCapsule and separately sealed deterministic structural comparison only; dedicated ReflectionReport ArtifactRefs are unresolved and attached only by the outer closure; upstream ArtifactRefs remain opaque and uninterpreted; no model, rule or world-state evaluation, effect replay, history rewrite, authorization, persistence, PDP or controller; all thirty-two replay attestations are false)`.

All four new objects have the same exact thirty-two false attestations: approval authentication, attempt-history completeness, authority, authorization, binding authentication, capsule completeness, CAS, completion, content provenance, effect, evaluation execution, evaluator independence, event append, execution, external-history resolution, Grant authentication, hard guard, instruction, outcome, permission, persistence, principal authentication, reflection completeness, replay equivalence, result authentication, rule evaluation, source resolution, transition, truth, usage measurement, verifier independence and world-state resolution. The embedded ADR-0090 objects retain their own exact twenty-two-attestation vocabulary; this decision neither widens nor rewrites it.

Each identity is SHA-256 over its NUL-terminated domain followed by compact canonical JSON with only that object's own ID and digest empty. Domains are `forgeos.aadm.structural-replay-manifest.v1`, `forgeos.aadm.decision-capsule.v1`, `forgeos.aadm.evaluation-branch.v1` and `forgeos.aadm.structural-replay-closure.v1`. Prefixes are `structural-replay-manifest-`, `decision-capsule-`, `evaluation-branch-` and `structural-replay-closure-`. Sealing requires both own identity fields blank. Nested blank identities, one-field blanks, stale nested seals and cross-capsule substitutions fail closed. Both the blank preimage and final sealed canonical document are independently measured.

Document ceilings are 4 MiB for a manifest, 26 MiB for a capsule, 64 KiB for a branch and 28 MiB for the outer closure. Shared limits remain depth sixteen, sixty-four object fields, 256 array items and 16 KiB UTF-8 strings. Structural manifest arrays admit zero through 256 ArtifactRefs, zero through sixty-four ArtifactReceipt refs, one through sixty-four Invocation refs, zero through 256 Event refs, one through sixty-four ExecutionReceipt refs and a combined one through 256 atom refs. ADR-0088 receipt coverage makes at most sixty-four distinct artifacts reachable from a semantically valid closure, but the manifest's independent shape ceiling remains 256.

The byte ceilings do not narrow the upstream contract. A conservative manifest shape upper envelope simultaneously uses sixty-four distinct ArtifactRefs with all 4,096 `artifact_ref` bytes maximally escaped and uniqueness supplied by their digests, sixty-four receipt refs, sixty-four invocation refs, 256 event refs, sixty-four execution refs and all 256 atom refs in the longer permitted predecision partition. Its blank canonical preimage is 684,285 bytes and its sealed upper bound is 684,440 bytes, below 4 MiB. A shape-only 256-ArtifactRef specimen is 2,218,274 bytes, proving 2 MiB was insufficient and 4 MiB preserves the frozen structural ceiling. With the ADR-0090 20 MiB closure ceiling, capsule fixed wrapper overhead of 1,867 bytes gives a conservative sealed bound of 21,657,827 bytes. A branch is at most 2,305 bytes. Thirty-two distinct maximally escaped reflection refs occupy at most 266,657 bytes because their digests supply uniqueness without shortening `artifact_ref`; with 2,083 bytes of outer fixed overhead, the outer conservative bound is 21,928,872 bytes. These bounds are below 26 MiB, 64 KiB and 28 MiB respectively.

Python exposes pure canonical, derive, digest, seal, validate and decode APIs. Public manifest validation and decoding require the sealed ADR-0090 closure; public branch validation and decoding require the sealed capsule. Capsule and outer validation recursively validate their complete dependencies exactly once per layer and then use validated internal candidate and digest paths, avoiding recursive revalidation growth. The local canonical encoder has its own exact 28 MiB bound rather than borrowing the smaller ADR-0090 document bound. Python and Rust measure deterministic emitted JSON against that byte ceiling before recursively enforcing the remaining profile, so compact shared aliases or large deterministic serializers cannot defer the byte rejection behind an unbudgeted profile walk.

Manifest and branch APIs reject their complete local shape before validating or cloning the larger supplied dependency. Outer closure APIs likewise reject constants, attestations and the zero-through-thirty-two dedicated reflection-reference array before traversing the embedded capsule, and derivation validates that array before touching the capsule. Local-invalid inputs therefore cannot force unrelated 20–26 MiB dependency work.

The Python checker accepts either the pinned physical LF-framed golden or one explicitly named canonical outer-closure file. Explicit files must be single-link regular files, are opened no-follow and are bounded before reading; absent no-follow support fails closed. `--golden` permits the fixture's single physical trailing LF and binds the bytes to the pin. `--file` accepts exact canonical JSON without framing whitespace, so passing the physical LF-framed golden to `--file` fails. Rejection writes no stdout; success writes only the exact outer result plus LF.

The Draft 2020-12 Schema is a structural shadow. Runtime validators remain the load-bearing accept set for canonical bytes, byte ceilings, exact upstream reseal, complete projections, order, identities, digest bindings and DAG relations. No Registry entry, scaffold distribution, product command, runtime adapter, authority service, external-history resolver, effect executor, persistence surface, PDP or controller is introduced.

## Consequences
Callers can package an exact ADR-0090 decision closure, reproduce a complete reference inventory and independently compare the sealed references without dropping unsuccessful attempts. Cross-language implementations can reproduce four domain-separated identities and one outer physical golden.

Reflection evidence remains attachable without creating a digest cycle: a ReflectionReport may cite a capsule, while only a later outer closure owns the dedicated report-attachment inventory. The capsule itself remains stable regardless of later reports. Upstream ArtifactRefs, including any caller-supplied `reflection_report` label, remain opaque and make no report-existence or report-binding claim.

The contract deliberately reports less than a replay engine. All thirty-two new attestations stay false, the branch has no candidate payload, and the only positive comparison is exact structural reference equality. A structurally valid closure does not establish completeness of external history, truth, authority, result authenticity, rule evaluation, world-state resolution, evaluator independence or replay equivalence.

A separate governance and source-distribution decision is still required before treating this Proposed core as distributed or closing any roadmap item. Runtime replay, authorization, history mutation and persistence require later decisions with their own authenticated evidence.

## Limitations
ADR-0092 remains Proposed. Its results mean only that supplied canonical objects validate, the embedded ADR-0090 closure exactly reseals, the manifest exactly projects that closure, and the separately sealed branch binds the same references. The contract does not execute or simulate a model, rule, capability or effect; resolve external history, a ReflectionReport, world state or policy; authenticate a source, principal, authority, Grant, Approval or result; establish evaluator independence, replay equivalence or completeness; authorize anything; rewrite history; append an event; persist a record; or operate a PDP or controller.

The manifest's 256-ArtifactRef shape allowance is not a claim that an ADR-0090 semantic closure can contain 256 distinct artifacts. Exact ADR-0088 receipt coverage and the sixty-four receipt ceiling reduce the reachable distinct set to sixty-four. The larger structural ceiling remains frozen for shape consistency and future diagnostics, while public semantic validation always requires and compares the complete closure.

Dedicated outer ReflectionReport ArtifactRefs are unresolved opaque declarations. Their presence does not prove a report exists, binds the capsule correctly, is complete or is authentic. Upstream ArtifactRefs are likewise uninterpreted even when a caller uses the same lexical kind. This core never reads a report and never permits the dedicated later-report attachment to feed back into the capsule digest.

The checker is a local conformance boundary, not a product capability or trusted ingestion service. The Schema does not replace runtime validation. No consumer is integrated, no existing ADR-0088 or ADR-0090 byte is changed, and no persisted state requires migration or rollback.
