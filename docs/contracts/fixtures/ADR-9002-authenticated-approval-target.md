# ADR-9002: Authenticated Approval Target Fixture

## Context
A deterministic proposal is needed to exercise exact physical, body and self binding without relying on repository discovery, identity assertions or an accepted lifecycle.

## Decision
Propose a fixture-only architecture change whose bytes may be supplied to the Authenticated Architecture Decision Approval v1 prerequisite structural checker.

## Consequences
The fixture gives tests one strict Proposed ADR v2 target. Its declared owners and approvers remain unauthenticated metadata, and structural approval relations do not make the decision accepted.

## Validation
Recompute the strict ADR v2 body, self and physical digests and reject any byte, filename, status, owner, approver or section mutation.

## Limitations
This fixture is not an accepted decision, trust root, policy activation, authenticated approval, authorization, persistence record, effect or production authority.
