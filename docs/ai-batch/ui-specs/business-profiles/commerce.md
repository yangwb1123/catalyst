# Commerce Profile

## Goal

Help customers discover products, understand price and availability, complete
checkout, and recover safely from payment, inventory, and network failures.

## Layout and density

- Standard density for catalog and account pages; compact density for merchant
  operations pages.
- Make product identity, price, promotion, inventory state, delivery promise,
  and primary purchase action explicit.
- Keep cart totals and checkout progress visible without obscuring details.

## Interaction requirements

- Cart and checkout changes recalculate totals with visible explanations.
- Prevent duplicate order and payment submissions.
- Distinguish sold out, price changed, promotion expired, payment failed, and
  payment result unknown states.
- Preserve cart and form input after recoverable failures.
